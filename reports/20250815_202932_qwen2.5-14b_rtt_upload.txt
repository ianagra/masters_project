## QoS Assessment Report: rtt_upload Approach

### 1. Executive Summary

This report presents an analysis of Quality of Service (QoS) issues identified through the statistical detection of change-points in network performance metrics, specifically focusing on the 'rtt_upload' approach. The study reveals significant differences between two clusters defined by stability and performance characteristics: Cluster 0 (High-Risk Profile - Unstable) and Cluster 1 (Low-Risk Profile - Stable). Key findings include critical clients and servers associated with instability, as well as influential metrics that predict network conditions. Recommendations are provided for targeted interventions to improve QoS.

### 2. Detailed Findings

#### Critical Clients
- **Client09**: Experienced significant performance degradation during interactions with `rnp_sp` and `gru_rj`, characterized by high RTT and low throughput.
- **Client12**: Showed intermittent instability, particularly in interactions with `rnp_sp`.
- **Client02**: Demonstrated periods of poor performance when interacting with `rnp_rj`.

#### Critical Server
- **Server rnp_rj**: Experienced prolonged periods of instability during interactions with `client16`, characterized by high RTT and throughput variability.

#### Influential Metrics
The following metrics were found to have the greatest impact on network stability:
- **RTT (Round Trip Time)**: High values indicate potential routing inefficiencies or congestion.
- **Throughput Variability**: Increased standard deviation in throughput (`throughput_download_std`, `throughput_upload_std`) is associated with unstable periods.

### 3. Recommended Actions

- **Conduct Traceroute Analysis**:
    - Perform traceroutes from critical clients (e.g., `client09`, `client12`, and `client02`) to problematic servers (`rnp_sp` and `gru_rj`) during unstable intervals.
    - Analyze the network path for any unexpected hops or delays that may cause increased latency.

- **Investigate Routing Issues**:
    - Focus on identifying specific network segments causing high RTT values, particularly between clients and servers like `client09-rnp_sp`, `client12-rnp_sp`, and `client02-rnp_rj`.
    - Optimize routing configurations to mitigate congestion or misrouting issues.

- **Monitor Performance Metrics**:
    - Continuously monitor RTT and throughput variability for critical clients and servers.
    - Implement alerts for significant deviations in these metrics to proactively address potential QoS issues.

By focusing on these actions, the operations team can effectively pinpoint and resolve network performance issues impacting QoS.
