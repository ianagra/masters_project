# QoS Assessment Report: throughput_download Approach

## Executive Summary

This report evaluates the Quality of Service (QoS) issues in the network using the throughput_download approach. The analysis identifies two distinct clusters of network performance: Cluster 0, associated with high risk and instability, and Cluster 1, characterized by stability and optimal performance. The report highlights critical elements, including specific clients and servers, that are strongly linked to the high-risk cluster. Detailed diagnoses of these elements reveal patterns of performance degradation, suggesting potential network congestion or configuration issues at specific sites.

## Detailed Findings

### Cluster Profile Analysis

- **Cluster 0 (High-Risk Profile)**: Characterized by shorter average interval durations, high event frequency, and low survival probability over time. Throughput performance is suboptimal, with lower download and upload means, and latency performance is poor, with higher RTT values.

- **Cluster 1 (Low-Risk Profile)**: Exhibits longer average interval durations, low event frequency, and high survival probability. Throughput performance is superior, with higher download and upload means, and latency performance is better, with lower RTT values.

### Critical Element Identification

- **client02**: Exhibits a strong association with Cluster 0, indicating frequent disruptions and poor network performance, particularly in interactions with `gru03`, `gru05`, and `gru06`.

- **client07**: Shows a mixed performance profile, with periods of both stability and instability. Notable issues are observed at `gru03`, while interactions with `gru05`, `gru06`, and `rnp_sp` are generally stable.

- **rnp_rj**: Predominantly associated with Cluster 1, indicating sustained instability. High variability in throughput and RTT metrics suggests potential routing or congestion issues.

- **rnp_sp**: Experiences a mix of stable and unstable periods, with high variability in throughput and RTT metrics during high-risk intervals. Multiple clients are affected, indicating widespread performance issues.

### Interval Diagnoses

- **client02**: High-risk intervals are linked to interactions with `gru03`, `gru05`, and `gru06`, suggesting potential network congestion or configuration issues at these sites.

- **client07**: Performance degradation at `gru03` suggests localized network issues, while interactions with other sites are generally stable.

- **rnp_rj**: High RTT and throughput variability suggest potential routing congestion or bottleneck issues, especially with clients like Client03, Client06, and Client09.

- **rnp_sp**: High RTT during interactions with multiple clients suggests potential routing issues or network congestion.

## Recommended Actions

- **Conduct targeted diagnostics** at `gru03`, `gru05`, and `gru06` to identify potential network congestion or configuration issues affecting `client02`.

- **Perform `traceroute` analyses** from `client07` to `gru03` to identify routing anomalies or bottlenecks causing performance degradation.

- **Investigate potential routing congestion** or bottleneck issues affecting `rnp_rj` by conducting `traceroute` analyses with clients like Client03, Client06, and Client09.

- **Examine network routing or congestion** issues affecting `rnp_sp` by performing `traceroute` analyses with multiple clients to identify problematic network hops or bottlenecks.

These actions aim to pinpoint and resolve the underlying causes of the observed performance degradation, thereby improving the overall network QoS.
