# **QoS Assessment Report: rtt_upload Approach**  

---

## **1. Executive Summary**  
This report evaluates the Quality of Service (QoS) of network interactions using the **rtt_upload approach**, which measures round-trip time (RTT) for upload operations. Key findings reveal persistent instability in client-server communications, with **Client02** and **Client07** exhibiting high-risk intervals (Cluster 0) linked to anomalies (event 1). Both clients show disproportionate activity with servers **rnp_rj** and **rnp_sp**, which require urgent investigation. Prolonged instability (e.g., 81.9 days at **gru06**) and transient high-risk intervals (e.g., 1.09 units at **rnp_rj**) highlight critical gaps in network reliability. Immediate action is recommended to address root causes and optimize performance.  

---

## **2. Detailed Findings**  

### **Client02: High-Risk Cluster Dominance**  
- **Cluster Distribution**:  
  - **Cluster 0 (High-Risk)**: 7 intervals (58.3% of total time), all tied to **event 1** (anomalies/errors).  
  - **Cluster 1 (Low-Risk)**: 5 intervals (41.7% of total time), associated with **event 0** (normal behavior).  
- **Key Servers**:  
  - **rnp_rj**: 6 intervals (50% of total), with **4 in Cluster 0** (high-risk, event 1).  
  - **rnp_sp**: 5 intervals (41.7% of total), with **3 in Cluster 0** (high-risk, event 1).  
- **Critical Observations**:  
  - **rnp_rj** and **rnp_sp** dominate interactions but show **66.7% and 60% high-risk activity**, respectively.  
  - Short, high-risk intervals (e.g., 1.09 units) may indicate transient failures, while long, low-risk intervals (e.g., 38.39 units) suggest stable but extended normal behavior.  

### **Client07: Prolonged Instability**  
- **Cluster Distribution**:  
  - **Cluster 1 (Instability)**: 5 intervals (83% of total time), linked to **event 1** (anomalies).  
  - **Cluster 0 (Stability)**: 1 interval (17% of total time), associated with **event 0** (normal behavior).  
- **Key Servers**:  
  - **rnp_rj**: 2 intervals (Cluster 1), indicating recurring performance issues.  
  - **gru06**: 1 interval (Cluster 1, 81.9 days), the longest instability period, suggesting persistent hardware or configuration issues.  
  - **rnp_sp**: 1 interval (Cluster 1), with a brief stable period (7 days in Cluster 0).  
- **Critical Observations**:  
  - **gru06** exhibits the longest instability, potentially due to hardware failure or sustained high load.  
  - **rnp_sp** shows intermittent instability, requiring investigation into transient network disruptions.

## **3. Recommended Actions**  

- **Investigate High-Risk Servers**:  
  - Conduct **network diagnostics** and **server health checks** for **rnp_rj** and **rnp_sp** to identify recurring issues (e.g., latency, misconfigurations).  
  - Prioritize **short, high-risk intervals** (e.g., 1.09 units) for root cause analysis, as they may indicate critical, transient failures.  

- **Address Prolonged Instability**:  
  - **gru06** (81.9 days in Cluster 1): Review hardware, load balancing, and upstream provider performance to resolve persistent issues.  
  - **rnp_rj** (Client02 and Client07): Implement **monitoring tools** to track recurring anomalies and optimize resource allocation.  

- **Leverage Stable Intervals for Benchmarking**:  
  - Analyze the **7-day stable interval** at **rnp_sp** (Cluster 0) to identify configurations or conditions that mitigate instability, informing remediation strategies for other sites.  

- **Enhance QoS Monitoring**:  
  - Deploy **real-time rtt_upload monitoring** to detect anomalies early and correlate them with server-specific events (event 0/1).  
  - Integrate **automated alerts** for high-risk clusters (Cluster 0) to enable proactive troubleshooting.  

- **Validate Server Configurations**:  
  - Cross-check configurations for **rnp_rj**, **rnp_sp**, and **gru06** to ensure alignment with QoS benchmarks and eliminate mismatches.  

---  

**Conclusion**: The rtt_upload approach has identified critical QoS gaps, particularly with **rnp_rj**, **rnp_sp**, and **gru06**. Immediate technical interventions are required to stabilize network performance and prevent recurring anomalies.
