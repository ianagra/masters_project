# QoS Assessment Report: rtt_upload Approach

## Executive Summary

This report presents a comprehensive analysis of network performance using the rtt_upload approach, focusing on the identification of instability patterns and critical elements affecting Quality of Service (QoS). The analysis reveals two distinct clusters: Cluster 0, characterized by high-risk and unstable network performance, and Cluster 1, indicating low-risk and stable conditions. Key elements, including specific clients and servers, were identified as contributing to the high-risk profile, with detailed diagnoses provided for each. The report concludes with actionable insights aimed at addressing the identified issues to enhance overall network stability and performance.

## Detailed Findings

### Cluster Profile Analysis

- **Cluster 0**: Assigned a high-risk profile due to its short mean interval duration and high event frequency, indicating frequent instability. The cluster exhibited poor throughput and high RTT values, reflecting suboptimal network performance.

- **Cluster 1**: Assigned a low-risk profile, characterized by longer interval durations and low event frequency, suggesting stable network conditions. This cluster demonstrated superior throughput and lower RTT values, indicative of optimal performance.

### Critical Element Identification

- **client09**: Exhibited a strong association with Cluster 0, indicating frequent instability and poor performance.
- **rnp_rj**: Showed a significant connection to the high-risk Cluster 0, suggesting potential issues with connection stability.
- **client12**: Displayed a considerable tendency towards instability, as indicated by its association with Cluster 0.
- **client02**: Demonstrated a notable connection to the unstable characteristics of Cluster 0.
- **client07**: Aligned significantly with the high-risk profile of Cluster 0, reflecting a pattern of instability.

### Individual Diagnoses

- **client09**: Predominantly stable but experienced degradation when interacting with site `gru03`, suggesting potential localized issues.

- **rnp_rj**: Experienced a mix of stable and unstable intervals, with high throughput variability and elevated RTT values during interactions with certain clients, indicating potential routing issues.

- **client12**: Exhibited both stable and unstable periods, with high-risk intervals associated with site `gru03`, indicating potential network congestion or routing inefficiencies.

- **client02**: Predominantly associated with high-risk intervals, with interactions with `rnp_rj` and `rnp_sp` showing elevated RTT and throughput variability, suggesting potential routing issues.

- **client07**: Displayed a higher proportion of time in high-risk intervals, with potential localized issues identified during interactions with `gru03`.

## Recommended Actions

- Conduct `traceroute` analyses from identified critical clients (`client09`, `client12`, `client02`, `client07`) to problematic sites (`gru03`, `rnp_rj`) to identify routing inefficiencies or bottlenecks.
- Investigate potential network congestion or routing inefficiencies at the `gru03` site using diagnostic tools to resolve increased RTT and reduced throughput.
- Examine network configurations and traffic patterns during high-risk intervals to identify underlying causes of instability.
- Implement targeted interventions to address identified routing anomalies and improve overall network performance and stability.
