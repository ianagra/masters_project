## QoS Assessment Report: throughput_download Approach

### 1) Executive Summary
Two behavioral clusters were identified. Cluster 1 (low-risk) exhibits longer, stable intervals with higher throughput and lower RTTs; Cluster 0 (high-risk) shows short, unstable intervals with degraded throughput and elevated RTTs. Logistic regression indicates download-path RTT (mean and jitter) is the dominant factor driving instability toward Cluster 0. The most critical clients are client02, client07, and client13; the most critical server is rnp_rj, with a mid-June to late-July degradation window affecting multiple clients and a persistent issue for client02.

### 2) Detailed Findings

- Cluster profiles
  - Cluster 1 — Stable (Low-Risk): Long mean duration (~24.3 days), very low event frequency (0.078), survival ≥0.79 up to 90 days; higher throughput (DL ~632 Mbps; UL ~684 Mbps); low RTTs (DL ~16 ms; UL ~19 ms).
  - Cluster 0 — Unstable (High-Risk): Short mean duration (~10.6 days), very high event frequency (0.738), steep survival decay by day 15–30; lower throughput (DL ~452 Mbps; UL ~429 Mbps); high RTTs (DL ~60 ms; UL ~61 ms).

- Most critical clients
  - client02 (coef −1.952): Very strong association with Cluster 0. 22/26 intervals unstable, entirely Cluster 0 toward rnp_rj and rnp_sp; stable only toward GRU targets. Elevated rtt_download_mean toward rnp_rj; to rnp_sp, Cluster 0 persists despite low RTT, indicating influence from non-RTT features and/or throughput variability.
  - client07 (coef −1.734): Strong association with Cluster 0. Unstable to gru03 until 2024-07-17 with extremely high RTT; becomes unstable to rnp_sp (from 2024-06-12) and rnp_rj (from 2024-06-16) driven by growing throughput volatility and RTT jitter (for rnp_sp).
  - client13 (coef −0.606): Moderate association with Cluster 0. Prolonged high RTT/jitter to gru03 through 2024-07-09; mid-July transients to rnp_rj and rnp_sp show Cluster 0 with low RTT but depressed download throughput.

- Most critical servers
  - rnp_rj (coef −1.368): Strong tendency toward Cluster 0. Although 54.7% of intervals are stable overall, a clear multi-client degradation period spans mid-June to late July (clients 07, 08, 04, 09, 13, 14, 16), plus a persistent issue for client02 across the entire observation.

- Metrics with greatest impact on stability (coefficients are log-odds of Cluster 1; negative → more likely Cluster 0)
  - rtt_download_mean (−1.454): Strongest driver. Higher download RTT sharply increases risk (e.g., high RTT phases for gru03 and several rnp_rj intervals).
  - rtt_download_std (−0.994): Higher download RTT jitter correlates with instability (e.g., spikes preceding/within Cluster 0 intervals).
  - rtt_upload_std (+0.897): Higher upload RTT jitter increases odds of stability in this dataset; Cluster 0 often shows lower rtt_upload_std.
  - rtt_upload_mean (−0.624): Elevated upload RTT moderately increases risk.
  - Note: While change-points were defined on throughput_download, the model’s cluster separation is predominantly explained by RTT levels/jitter, with throughput drops/volatility contributing in specific episodes (e.g., client07/13 toward RNP in mid-July).

- Targeted interval diagnoses (highlights)
  - client02: All rnp_rj and rnp_sp intervals unstable across May–July; GRU targets stable. Elevated rtt_download_mean toward rnp_rj; rnp_sp shows Cluster 0 despite healthy RTTs, aligning with non-RTT influences and occasional throughput volatility.
  - client07: Severe RTT inflation to gru03 until 2024-07-17; from mid-June, rnp_sp/rnp_rj shift to Cluster 0 due to throughput volatility (and increased DL-RTT jitter for rnp_sp), despite low mean RTT to rnp_rj.
  - client13: High RTT/jitter to gru03 until early July; mid-July RNP intervals show low RTT but depressed download throughput during Cluster 0.
  - rnp_rj server: Multi-client degradation from mid-June to late July marked by increased DL-RTT mean/jitter and, for some clients, throughput volatility; client02 remains persistently unstable toward rnp_rj across the whole period.

### 3) Recommended Actions

- Path and latency localization
  - Run synchronized traceroute/Paris-traceroute and MTR from client02 and client07 (plus 08, 13, 14, 16) to rnp_rj and rnp_sp during peak and off-peak; identify the hop(s)/segment(s) where DL-RTT mean/jitter increases.
  - Request reverse-path traceroute from rnp_rj/rnp_sp back to affected clients to check for path asymmetry.

- Throughput degradation analysis (RNP focus starting mid-June)
  - Execute iperf3 with multiple parallel streams toward rnp_rj and rnp_sp; capture retransmissions/loss and per-stream variability.
  - Correlate NDT throughput variance with interface utilization, queue depth, and drops on peering/aggregation toward RNP; verify if any shaping/policers were introduced around 2024-06-12/16.

- Persistent client02 issue
  - Compare paths and per-hop latency to rnp_rj/rnp_sp versus stable GRU targets; isolate the earliest diverging hop/AS/IX segment associated with elevated DL-RTT to RNP.
  - Validate if “event=1” intervals map to operational changes or maintenance windows that align with instability.

- GRU03 historical RTT anomaly (client07, client13)
  - Review BGP/IGP changes and interface utilization from 2024-05 to 2024-07-17; confirm the routing/queueing root cause of the elevated RTT/jitter and ensure post-07-17 path remains in the normalized state.

- Server-side checks (rnp_rj)
  - Inspect device/port counters, queue/CPU, and traffic patterns from 2024-06-15 to 2024-07-23 for congestion signatures.
  - Validate upstream/IX paths during the degradation window; coordinate with peers for any known incidents.

- Monitoring hardening
  - Add continuous DL-RTT mean/std probes per client-server pair with alerting thresholds aligned to model drivers (e.g., DL-RTT mean and std).
  - Track throughput_download mean/std jointly with RTT to capture both RTT-driven and throughput-variance-driven instabilities.
