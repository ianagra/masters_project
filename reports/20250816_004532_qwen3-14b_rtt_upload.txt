# QoS Assessment Report: rtt_upload Approach  

---

## 1. Executive Summary  
The QoS assessment of the rtt_upload server reveals persistent instability, with 94.1% of observed intervals falling into **Cluster 1** (unstable performance). **Client16** is identified as the most critical client, contributing to 66.7% of high-risk intervals due to extreme variability in upload throughput (std: 132.82) and latency (std: 10.21). Key metrics such as throughput standard deviation and RTT fluctuations are strongly correlated with instability, pointing to potential network congestion, hardware issues, or misconfigurations. Immediate investigation of client16’s connections is recommended to mitigate risks.

## 3. Recommended Actions  
- **Investigate Client16’s Network and Configuration**:  
  - Conduct packet loss and latency tests between client16 and the server.  
  - Review client16’s hardware and software configurations for anomalies.  

- **Monitor High-Variance Metrics**:  
  - Continuously track upload/download throughput and RTT standard deviations for client16 and other clients.  
  - Use tools like Wireshark or network analyzers to identify congestion patterns.  

- **Optimize Server Configuration**:  
  - Re-evaluate QoS settings to prioritize critical clients (e.g., client16).  
  - Check for server-side bottlenecks (e.g., CPU, memory, or disk I/O limitations).  

- **Implement Redundancy and Failover Mechanisms**:  
  - Deploy backup routes or load balancers to mitigate single points of failure.  
  - Test failover protocols during peak load scenarios.  

- **Conduct Root Cause Analysis**:  
  - Correlate instability intervals with server logs, client activity, and external network events (e.g., DDoS attacks, ISP outages).
