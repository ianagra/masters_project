# QoS Assessment Report: throughput_download Approach  

---

## 1. Executive Summary  
This assessment identifies **intermittent instability** in network performance, with **Cluster 1 (high-risk)** intervals dominating the dataset. Key findings include:  
- **Client13** experienced severe instability linked to **rnp_rj** and **rnp_sp**, marked by **50–70% throughput_download drops** and **25% higher RTT_download** during high-risk intervals.  
- **RTT_download** and **throughput_download** are the most critical metrics, with elevated RTT and reduced throughput strongly correlated with instability.  
- **Client14** and **Client16** exhibited variable behavior, transitioning between stable and unstable states.  
- **rnp_rj** and **rnp_sp** were consistently associated with high-risk intervals, suggesting potential network or server-side anomalies.  

---

## 2. Detailed Findings  

### **Critical Clients**  
- **Client13**:  
  - Most frequent and severe instability (5 high-risk intervals with **rnp_rj**, 1 with **rnp_sp**).  
  - Sharp throughput_download drops (e.g., 248.61 Mbps vs. 580.32 Mbps in stable intervals).  
- **Client14** and **Client16**:  
  - Variable behavior, with intervals in both **Cluster 0** (stable) and **Cluster 1** (unstable).  
  - Suggests transient issues or external factors influencing stability.  

### **Critical Servers**  
- **rnp_rj**:  
  - Linked to **5/10 high-risk intervals**, with **RTT_download peaking at 22.42 ms** (vs. 19.87 ms in stable intervals).  
  - Potential routing anomalies or congestion.  
- **rnp_sp**:  
  - **1/3 high-risk intervals**, marked by **50% throughput_download drops** (173.97 Mbps vs. 596.05 Mbps in stable intervals).  
  - Brief instability during 2024-07-17–19.  

### **Metrics with Greatest Impact on Stability**  
- **RTT_download**:  
  - **25% higher** in high-risk intervals (e.g., 22.42 ms at **rnp_rj**).  
  - Strong correlation with instability, indicating latency bottlenecks.  
- **Throughput_download**:  
  - **50–70% drops** during high-risk intervals (e.g., 248.61 Mbps vs. 580.32 Mbps at **rnp_rj**).  
  - Suggests congestion or resource contention.  
- **Event 1**:  
  - Associated with **40% of high-risk intervals**, possibly maintenance or external disruptions.  

---

## 3. Recommended Actions  
- **Investigate rnp_rj and rnp_sp**:  
  - Run **traceroute** between **Client13** and **rnp_rj** to identify latency bottlenecks.  
  - Cross-check **rnp_rj** server logs for simultaneous congestion events or hardware failures.  
- **Monitor rnp_sp during 2024-07-17–19**:  
  - Analyze recurring patterns in throughput_download and RTT_download.  
- **Expand Analysis to Client14 and Client16**:  
  - Investigate external factors (e.g., application usage, local network conditions) influencing their variable behavior.  
- **Implement Real-Time Monitoring**:  
  - Track **RTT_download** and **throughput_download** for **rnp_rj** and **rnp_sp** to detect anomalies early.  
- **Validate Event 1 Correlation**:  
  - Confirm if **Event 1** (e.g., maintenance, congestion) is a recurring cause of instability.
