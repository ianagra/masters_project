## QoS Assessment Report: rtt_upload Approach

**1. Executive Summary:**

This report details the analysis of RTT (Round Trip Time) upload data collected from network probes, utilizing a change-point detection approach to identify periods of unstable network behavior. The analysis revealed that clients `client09`, `client12`, `client02`, `client07`, and server `rnp_rj` are strongly associated with periods of degraded performance (Cluster 0). These findings suggest potential network connectivity issues or capacity constraints impacting these specific connections, warranting further investigation and targeted remediation efforts.

**2. Detailed Findings:**

The analysis categorized network performance intervals into two clusters based on RTT upload metrics and associated features. Cluster 0 was identified as the high-risk profile, characterized by short interval durations, rapidly declining stability, and elevated RTT values. Conversely, Cluster 1 represented a low-risk, stable profile with longer interval durations and lower RTT values. 

Logistic regression analysis highlighted `client09`, `client12`, `client02`, `client07`, and `rnp_rj` as key drivers of Cluster 0 membership, indicating a strong association with unstable network behavior. 

*   **client09:** Exhibits generally stable performance but with significant variation in interval duration. The long interval lengths mask potential short-term instabilities.
*   **client12:** Demonstrates mostly stable performance, but a smaller proportion of intervals show performance variations, particularly in RTT.
*   **client02:** Exhibits a mixed performance profile, with a significant portion of intervals falling into the high-risk Cluster 0.
*   **client07:** Shows mostly stable performance, but a smaller proportion of intervals show performance variations, particularly in RTT.
*   **rnp_rj:** Consistently appears in intervals associated with degraded performance, particularly in conjunction with `client02`, `client07`, `client09` and `client12`. 

Further investigation revealed that the elevated RTT values associated with these clients and server often correlate with specific network paths. Specifically, `client09`, `client12`, `client02`, and `client07` consistently exhibit higher RTT values when interacting with `rnp_rj`, suggesting a potential bottleneck or capacity constraint along this path. The lack of consistent degradation across all sites suggests the issue is localized to these specific connections.

**3. Recommended Actions:**

*   **Network Path Analysis:** Conduct `traceroute` and `ping` tests from `client09`, `client12`, `client02`, and `client07` to `rnp_rj` to identify potential bottlenecks, increased latency, or routing issues.
*   **Capacity Assessment:** Evaluate the network capacity along the path between these clients and `rnp_rj` to determine if congestion is contributing to the elevated RTT values.
*   **Monitoring Implementation:** Implement continuous monitoring of RTT upload metrics specifically for interactions between these clients and `rnp_rj` to track performance trends and identify recurring issues.
*   **Configuration Review:** Review the network configuration of `client09`, `client12`, `client02`, and `client07` to ensure optimal routing and QoS settings.
*   **Correlation with Other Metrics:** Correlate RTT upload data with other network performance metrics (e.g., throughput, packet loss) to gain a more comprehensive understanding of the underlying issues.
