## QoS Assessment Report: throughput_download Approach

**1. Executive Summary:**

This report details a Quality of Service (QoS) assessment based on throughput download data, utilizing change-point detection and logistic regression to identify network instability. Analysis reveals that `client02`, `client07`, `rnp_rj`, and `rnp_sp` are key elements influencing network stability. Specifically, consistent performance issues are observed between these elements, particularly impacting throughput. The findings suggest potential routing or capacity issues between `client02`/`client07` and `rnp_rj`, as well as potential congestion affecting communication with `rnp_sp`. Addressing these issues is crucial for maintaining a stable and high-performing network.

**2. Detailed Findings:**

The analysis categorized network intervals into two clusters: Cluster 0 (Low-Risk/Stable) and Cluster 1 (High-Risk/Unstable). Cluster 1 is characterized by longer interval durations but decreasing survival probability, indicating a tendency towards instability despite initially higher throughput. Conversely, Cluster 0 exhibits shorter intervals but greater persistence, suggesting consistent, albeit potentially slower, connections.

Logistic regression identified `client02` and `client07` as strongly associated with the stable Cluster 0, indicating their consistent contribution to network stability. Conversely, `rnp_rj` and `rnp_sp` showed a tendency towards Cluster 1, suggesting they are more prone to instability.

Detailed diagnoses revealed specific performance patterns:

*   **client02:** Consistently exhibits stable throughput and RTT when interacting with `rnp_sp`, but experiences significantly lower throughput when interacting with `rnp_rj`. This suggests a potential network issue between these two elements.
*   **client07:** Shows a clear performance bifurcation based on site. Interactions with `rnp_sp` are generally stable, while interactions with `rnp_rj` consistently exhibit lower throughput and contribute to instability.
*   **rnp_rj:** Exhibits wide variance in throughput, suggesting inconsistent network conditions. Interactions with `client02`, `client07`, `client14`, `client15`, and `client16` appear to be key contributors to this variance.
*   **rnp_sp:** Shows a consistent pattern of throughput degradation, particularly during interactions with `client14`, `client15`, and `client16`. This suggests potential congestion or capacity issues affecting these connections.

The consistent performance differences observed between `client02`/`client07` and `rnp_rj`, coupled with the throughput degradation affecting communication with `rnp_sp`, highlight potential bottlenecks or suboptimal routing paths impacting network performance.

**3. Recommended Actions:**

*   **Investigate Network Path between client02 and rnp_rj:** Utilize `traceroute` and `ping` to identify potential bottlenecks or high-latency links. Compare the path with the one used for `rnp_sp`.
*   **Investigate Network Path between client07 and rnp_rj:** Perform `traceroute` and `ping` tests during peak and off-peak hours to identify potential bottlenecks or high-latency links. Check the capacity of the link between these two locations.
*   **Analyze Traffic Patterns to rnp_rj:** Monitor network traffic patterns during periods of high and low performance to correlate performance with network load.
*   **Investigate Traffic Patterns to rnp_sp:** Analyze network traffic patterns during interactions with `client14`, `client15`, and `client16` to identify potential congestion or capacity issues.
*   **Review Routing Configuration:** Examine the routing configuration between the identified clients and servers to ensure optimal path selection.
*   **Capacity Planning:** Evaluate the capacity of the network links between the identified clients and servers to ensure sufficient bandwidth for current and future demands.
